Greenfield University Admission Handbook

Admission Overview

This handbook guides applicants through the admission process, eligibility, documents, terms and fees for the academic session 2026–2027.

Eligibility

Candidates must have passed 10+2 with a minimum of 50% marks from a recognized board. Meeting the eligibility criteria is mandatory before applying.

Application Process

  1. Register on the admission portal
  2. Fill the online application form
  3. Upload required documents
  4. Pay the application fee
  5. Appear for entrance test / interview
  6. Check merit list and confirm admission

The last date to apply is 30th June 2026.

Required Documents

  • Previous mark sheets
  • Identity proof
  • Passport size photograph
  • Migration certificate
  • Category certificate (if applicable)

Important Terms

Eligibility
Minimum academic qualification required to apply for a program
Merit List
Ranked list of applicants published based on marks scored
Counselling
Process of seat allotment based on merit and preference
Scholarship
Financial aid granted to eligible meritorious students
Registration
Confirmation of admission after paying the fee
