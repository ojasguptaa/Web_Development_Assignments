Faculty Information

The department has experienced faculty members guiding students in theory and practical sessions.

Before a Practical Class

  1. Bring the practical file and record book
  2. Complete previous experiment write-up
  3. Reach the lab five minutes early
  4. Wear the ID card

Classroom Rules

  • Maintain silence during lectures
  • Switch off mobile phones
  • Attend a minimum of 75% classes
  • Carry the department notebook

Academic Rules

Lecture

Small group session for doubt clearing and problem solving
