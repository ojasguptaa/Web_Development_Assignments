Annual College Fest 2026

Fest Introduction

The Annual College Fest 2026 is a three-day celebration of talent, culture and technology, bringing together students from across the region.

Registration Process

  1. Visit the official fest website
  2. Select the desired event category
  3. Fill the registration form with team/individual details
  4. Pay the registration fee online
  5. Receive confirmation via email

Registration closes on 5th March 2026.

Rules

  • Valid college ID is mandatory for entry
  • Participants must report 30 minutes before the event
  • Only one entry per participant per category
  • Decision of judges will be final

Terms

Open Event
Event open to participants from any college
Team Event
Event requiring a group of participants to compete together
Individual Event
Event where a single participant competes independently
Registration
Process of enrolling for an event before the deadline
Coordinator
Faculty or student in-charge managing an event
Final Round
Last stage of competition deciding the winners
