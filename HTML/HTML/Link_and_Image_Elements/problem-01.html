About My College

GLA University is a private state university located in Greater Noida, Uttar Pradesh, established in 2010. It offers diploma, undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral programs across engineering, management, pharmacy, law, and sciences, holding an A+ grade from NAAC and 12B status from the UGC.

Size: Sprawls across 110+ acres of lush green environment.
Facilities: Over 150 advanced laboratories, separate hostels for boys and girls, extensive sports arenas, and innovation hubs.
Tech Collaborations: Features strategic tech partnerships, including initiatives aligned with Microsoft and Google for advanced digital and AI learning components.

Campus Life

Infrastructure and Facilities: Equipped with modern smart classrooms, innovation labs, and specialized coding or tech centers. On-campus cafes, canteens, and convenience stores provide relaxed spaces to unwind between classes.

Culture and Activities: Regular celebrations like Diwali Mela, Garba nights, national holiday programs, and annual cultural fests are organized for students. Active groups focusing on dance, music, drama, art, and fashion provide students with opportunities to participate in different activities.

Tech Culture: Frequent hackathons, guest lectures, technical workshops, and entrepreneurial conclaves keep the atmosphere dynamic. The environment also allows students to manage their coursework while developing technical skills and working on personal projects.
