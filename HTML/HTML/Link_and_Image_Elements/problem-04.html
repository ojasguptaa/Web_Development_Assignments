Agra Travel Destination Guide

Attractions

Agra is famous for the Taj Mahal and Agra Fort.

Culture

Agra has a rich Mughal heritage and is known for its historical monuments and traditional arts.

Food

Agra is known for its famous petha and traditional Mughlai cuisine.

Best Time

October to March is the recommended season to visit Agra.

Travel Tips

Travel early in the day to visit popular attractions and avoid crowds.
