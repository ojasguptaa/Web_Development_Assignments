Physics

Physics: Newton's Second Law states that force is equal to mass multiplied by acceleration. The formula is F = m × a. This law explains how force affects the motion of an object.

Newton's Second Law: F = m × a

Gravitational Force: Fg = Gm1m2 / r2

Einstein's Mass-Energy Relation: E = mc2

Chemistry

Chemistry: Water is a compound made of two hydrogen atoms and one oxygen atom, represented by H2O. The subscript 2 shows the number of hydrogen atoms in a water molecule.

Water: H2O

Carbon Dioxide: CO2

Oxygen: O2

Sulfuric Acid: H2SO4

Ammonia: NH3

Square Meter: m2

Cubic Meter: m3

Scientific Notation: 106

Biology

Biology: Photosynthesis is the process by which green plants make food using carbon dioxide and water in the presence of sunlight. The equation is 6CO2 + 6H2O → C6H12O6 + 6O2. Photosynthesis produces glucose and oxygen.

Photosynthesis: 6CO2 + 6H2O → C6H12O6 + 6O2

Cellular Respiration: C6H12O6 + 6O2 → 6CO2 + 6H2O

DNA: C5H10N5O

Cell Size: 10-6 m

DNA Base Pairing: A1 ↔ T1

Population Growth: N = N0(1 + r)t
