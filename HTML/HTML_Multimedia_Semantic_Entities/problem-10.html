Key Terms

Career Expo
A large event connecting job seekers directly with hiring organizations
Internship
A short-term, supervised work opportunity for students & graduates
Placement
Formal job offer & hiring process facilitated through campus or expo drives
Workshop
A hands-on training session focused on a specific skill
Certification
An official credential awarded upon completing a course or program
Networking
Building professional relationships with peers, mentors & recruiters
