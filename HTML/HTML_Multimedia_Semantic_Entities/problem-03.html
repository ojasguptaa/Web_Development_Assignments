Destination Overview

Meridian Hills is a breathtaking hill station known for its lush valleys, cool climate & rich culture. It is the perfect getaway for nature lovers & adventure seekers alike.

Top Attractions

From waterfalls to ancient temples, Meridian Hills offers something for every traveler.

Travel Tips

Carry warm clothing as temperatures can drop below 10°C at night. Always keep bottled water handy.

One-Day Itinerary

  1. Sunrise at Sunset Point
  2. Breakfast at local café
  3. Boating at Crystal Lake
  4. Lunch & rest
  5. Visit Old Stone Temple
  6. Evening at Pine Forest Trail
