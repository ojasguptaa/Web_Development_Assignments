Event Overview

The National Career & Education Fair 2026 brings students, job seekers, universities and leading organizations together under one platform. Attendees can explore career opportunities, internships, higher education and scholarships.

Objective: Connect talent with opportunities and help participants make informed career decisions.

"Your next opportunity could begin with one conversation." — Event Organizer

Date: 15–16 October 2026 | Time: 9:00 AM–6:00 PM

Preparation Guide

  • Carry multiple copies of your resume.
  • Research participating organizations.
  • Prepare a short introduction about yourself.
  • Dress appropriately for professional interactions.
  • Keep your questions ready.
  1. Register online.
  2. Reach the venue.
  3. Collect your attendee pass.
  4. Visit organizations and sessions.
  5. Network and follow up.

Preparation Tips & Notices

Tip: Arrive at least 30 minutes early and keep your registration confirmation ready.

Some sessions may have limited seating. Registration does not guarantee a seat in every session.
