Student Terms & Rules

General Rules

Students must carry their college ID card at all times.

Students must maintain cleanliness in classrooms and common areas.

Note: Students are expected to follow all instructions given by faculty members.

Attendance

Students must maintain the minimum required attendance.

Attendance below the required percentage may result in being debarred from examinations.

Examination

Students must carry their valid ID card during examinations.

Late entry after the permitted time may result in denial of entry to the examination hall.

Note: Students should follow all examination instructions carefully.
Mobile phones and unauthorized materials are not permitted.

Assignment Submission

All assignments must be submitted before the given deadline.

Late submissions may receive reduced marks or a penalty.

Discipline

Students must behave respectfully towards faculty members, staff, and other students.

Any form of misconduct or disruptive behavior is strictly prohibited.
