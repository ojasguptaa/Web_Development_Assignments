New Technology Center Opens at Greenfield University

New facility aims to give students better opportunities for technology and innovation

Greenfield University has opened a new technology center on its campus to provide students with modern facilities for learning, research and innovation. The center includes computer laboratories, collaborative workspaces and areas for technology projects.

The new facility is located at the university's main campus in Greater Noida, Uttar Pradesh. Students from different departments will be able to use the center for academic projects, workshops and technology-related activities.

The technology center officially opened on 24 August 2026 during a small campus event attended by university staff, students and faculty members. The opening ceremony included presentations about the new facilities and their planned use.

University officials said the center was created because technology skills are becoming increasingly important in modern education and employment. The facility is expected to encourage students to develop practical skills in programming, artificial intelligence and other emerging technologies.

***The university expects more than 1,000 students to use the facility during its first academic year.*** The center will also host workshops and student projects throughout the year.
