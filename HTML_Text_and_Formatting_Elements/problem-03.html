New Technology Park Opens in the City

New facility aims to support students and local innovators

A new technology park was opened in New Delhi on 23 August 2026, bringing together students, developers, and local innovators. The facility includes spaces for learning, teamwork, and technology projects.

Officials said that the technology park will provide free access to learning resources for students. Several workshops and training programs will be organized throughout the year to help young people develop technical skills.

The opening event was attended by students, teachers, and technology professionals. The new facility is expected to encourage more students to explore technology and innovation.
